📖 About biorhythm theory

Biorhythm theory began in 1897 with Austrian physician Wilhelm Fliess, who proposed three innate cycles — physical (23 days), emotional (28 days) and intellectual (33 days). Though dismissed by mainstream science, it remains popular as a light form of self‑reflection. Read more →
