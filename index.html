📖 About biorhythm theory

Biorhythm theory was introduced by Austrian physician Wilhelm Fliess in 1897. It proposes three innate cycles — physical (23 days), emotional (28 days) and intellectual (33 days) — that begin at birth. Although dismissed by mainstream science, the theory remains popular as a light form of self‑reflection. Read more on Wikipedia →
